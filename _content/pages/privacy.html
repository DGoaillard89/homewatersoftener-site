This page explains what happens to the details you give us through the quote form and the service booking form on this website. Short version: we use them to get back to you and sort out your job. It also covers the optional analytics and advertising cookies described under Cookies below.

Who we are

HomeWaterSoftener.co.uk is a trading name of DS Water, The Lightbox G16, 111 Power Road, London W4 5PY. DS Water is responsible (the "controller") for the personal data described here.

Got a question about your data? Call us on 020 3633 6064 (Mon–Fri 8am–6pm) or write to us at the address above.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or ask for a service appointment, we get:

  • your name, phone number, email address and postcode
  • what you're interested in or the service you need, your preferred date and time, your system's make or model, and anything you type in the message or notes box
  • if you used our postcode checker first, the estimated water hardness and softened-sodium figures for your area
  • if you arrived by clicking one of our online ads, the ad reference and campaign tags from the web address

The postcode checker itself runs in your browser. The postcode you type into it isn't sent to us unless you go on to send a form.

Why we use it

To reply to you, give you a quote, and arrange and carry out the survey, installation or service you asked about. Our lawful basis is taking steps you've asked for before a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who get in touch.

If you agree to analytics or advertising cookies, we use that information to understand how the site is used and how well our ads work. The lawful basis for that is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer.

We don't sell your details, and we won't add you to a marketing mailing list.

Where it's stored

When you press send, your form goes through a small secure relay run on Cloudflare and lands in our customer records system (Airtable). Both process the data on our behalf and may store it outside the UK, with the proper safeguards in place. Only the DS Water team who handle enquiries and bookings can see it.

How long we keep it

If you become a customer, we keep your details for as long as we look after your system, and for as long as we need them for our business and tax records. If your enquiry doesn't go anywhere, we delete it once we no longer need it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Just call us on 020 3633 6064 or drop us a line by post. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and similar technologies

We'd like to measure how people find and use this site, and how well our adverts work. To do that we may use Google Analytics and Google Ads, loaded through Google Tag Manager. These set cookies and similar identifiers in your browser, and Google receives information about your visit, such as the pages you view, the ad you clicked and your IP address.

None of this happens unless you say yes. When measurement is switched on, you'll see a cookie banner on your first visit. Until you choose, Google's tags aren't loaded at all. You can accept everything, reject everything, or pick:

  • Analytics: counts visits and shows us which pages are useful, so we can improve the site.
  • Advertising: lets Google Ads measure whether our ads led to an enquiry, and show our ads to people who've visited this site before, for example while they browse other websites.

We remember your choice in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so we don't ask on every page. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. If you arrive by clicking one of our ads, the ad reference in the web address (and any campaign tags) may be added to your enquiry message, so we can tell which ad brought you to us.

Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which see your IP address in the usual way. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure. The postcode hardness checker runs entirely in your browser and doesn't store anything.

Spam protection

Our forms include a hidden field that people never see but spam bots tend to fill in. If it's filled in, the message is quietly discarded. We don't use any tracking or fingerprinting to do this.
