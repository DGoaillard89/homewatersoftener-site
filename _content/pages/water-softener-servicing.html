A water softener is a pretty low-maintenance bit of kit. Keep it topped up with salt and it'll quietly get on with things. But it's still a machine with valves, seals and a resin bed doing hard work every day, and London water gives it plenty to do. A yearly service keeps it working properly, and if something's already gone wrong, we can usually put it right on the spot. We service and repair softeners across London and the Home Counties, whoever made yours.

What an annual service covers

Every softener's a bit different, but a typical service looks like this:

  • Testing the water coming in and going out, so we know the softener's actually softening.
  • Checking the settings, including the hardness setting and the clock, and adjusting them if your water or household has changed.
  • Cleaning the brine tank and clearing any salt bridges or sludge in the bottom.
  • Cleaning the injector and screens, the small parts that draw brine through. They're a common cause of trouble when they clog up.
  • Sanitising the resin to keep it clean and working well.
  • Checking valves, seals and connections for wear and leaks, including the bypass valve and drain.
  • Running a manual regeneration to make sure the whole cycle works from start to finish.

Not sure how often yours needs doing? Our post on how often to service a water softener covers it. The short answer for most homes is once a year.

Repairs and common faults

When a softener misbehaves, it's usually one of a handful of things. The most common ones we see:

  • Hard water's back. Limescale returning on taps and the kettle. Often a salt bridge, a blocked injector or the wrong settings.
  • Salt isn't going down. If the salt level hasn't moved in weeks, the softener probably isn't regenerating.
  • Water in the brine tank is too high. Usually a drain, injector or valve problem.
  • Leaks from connections, the bypass, or the valve itself.
  • Error codes or a blank display on electric units, sometimes after a power cut.
  • Low water pressure after the softener, which can point to a blocked filter or a valve issue.

Before you book, it's worth running through our water softener troubleshooting checklist. Some problems are a five-minute fix you can do yourself, and we'd rather you didn't pay for a visit you don't need.

RO, UV and carbon filter servicing

We look after drinking water systems as well as softeners:

  • Reverse osmosis (RO): we recommend a yearly service. We change the filters, check the membrane and the storage tank, and sanitise the system.
  • UV sterilisers: the lamp and quartz sleeve should be replaced every year. A UV lamp can still glow after it's stopped doing its job properly, so don't go by whether it's lit.
  • Under-sink carbon filters: cartridges typically need changing every 6–12 months, depending on how much you use the tap.

If you've got a softener and a drinking water system, we can usually do both on the same visit.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a water softener be serviced?

Once a year suits most homes. It keeps the settings right, the brine tank clean and the valve working, and it catches small problems before they turn into bigger ones.

Will you service a softener you didn't install?

Yes. We service and repair all major brands, including ones that came with the house. Just let us know the make and model if you can find it.

How much notice do you need?

Online bookings need at least 72 hours' notice, for any weekday. For Saturdays, or anything more urgent, get in touch through the enquiry form or give us a call.

My softener's stopped using salt. Is it broken?

Not necessarily. It's often a salt bridge, a blocked injector or a settings issue after a power cut. Try our troubleshooting checklist first, and if that doesn't sort it, book a visit.

Can you service my RO or UV system at the same time?

Yes. We can usually service softeners, reverse osmosis, UV and carbon filters on one visit. Just mention everything you've got when you book.
